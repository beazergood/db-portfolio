# Brand — Posture, Voice, Style

> Brand is *who* the project is. The [design system](./design-system.md) is *how that shows up* visually. This page is starting-frame, not finished doctrine — append over time, refresh when something stops fitting.

## Posture

- Senior contractor, not a junior. Not loud about it.
- **Calm-confident, not calm-passive.** Calm isn't softness; it's deliberate restraint. Opinionated when it counts.
- **Edgy, on the frontier, anti-template.** Cookie-cutter contractor portfolios are not the energy. Single bold idea executed with rare restraint, after [dcurt.is](https://dcurt.is/).
- **The medium is the message.** Where possible, the UI itself demonstrates the craft — interactions, typography, composition all do work that copy cannot.
- **Depth on demand.** The more a visitor interacts, the more they find. Nothing is laid out comprehensively by default. A 2-minute visit should feel right; a 20-minute visit rewards.
- Show, don't tell. Surface project facets rather than write a CV in prose.
- Human warmth via considered interaction, not just pastels and waves.

## Voice

- First-person, but understated.
- British spelling.
- Plain language. No jargon if a normal word will do.
- Self-deprecating without being insecure.
- Doesn't oversell.
- **No craft-romance vocabulary.** Words like *rig*, *bench*, *workshop*, *artisan*, *maker* don't fit. Prefer plain alternatives — *tools*, *using*, *day to day*, *building*. Dave sees himself as a developer who cobbles things together, not a craftsperson at a workbench.

## What we are

- A working canvas for one engineer's ideas, kept alive across years.
- A small, considered surface that returning visitors recognise.
- The kind of site that loads instantly on patchy mobile and never asks for cookies.

## What we are not

- **Not naming named clients or employers in copy.** Project work is described by *kind* (clinical portal, real-time video, sports-data platform) — never by *brand*. This is a hard rule, not a guideline.
- Not a blog (yet, maybe ever).
- Not an "about me" page that lists every job ever held.
- Not a CV. The CV lives elsewhere if needed; the canvas does something different.

## Inspiration / mood

> Loose pins, not a strict mood board. Add anchors as they emerge. When something stops fitting, replace it rather than rationalise.

- **[dcurt.is](https://dcurt.is/)** — calm, opinionated, anti-template, a single bold idea per page, no fluff. North star for the portfolio refresh.

## The April Fools foil

The `april-fools-2026` build is part of the brand by being its inverse. It proves the canonical site is *deliberately* calm — not because we don't know how to make noise, but because we choose not to.
